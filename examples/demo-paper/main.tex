\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

sshleaf edits LaTeX projects that live on your machine or on any SSH host,
and compiles them locally with Tectonic~\cite{tectonic}.
Double-click anywhere in the PDF to jump to the matching source line.

\section{Method}

We minimize a simple quadratic objective,
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}(\theta) = \frac{1}{2} \lVert A \theta - b \rVert_2^2,
  \qquad
  \theta^\star = (A^\top A)^{-1} A^\top b .
\end{equation}
